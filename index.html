About Me

I'm Sean Thomas Olson, a fashion designer and multidisciplinary creative interested in the relationship between clothing, character, and contemporary culture.

My process begins with observation and research, then moves through drawing, collage, fabric exploration, draping, and refinement. I look for the tension between structure and movement, restraint and expression, familiarity and surprise.

I approach each project as a complete visual world. Silhouette, fabrication, finish, styling, and presentation work together to communicate one clear point of view.

I am drawn to collaborative environments where ideas can evolve through critique, experimentation, and skilled making. The goal is work that feels distinctive, purposeful, and alive on the body.

Design Approach

01 — Discover
Research & Context
Build the visual world

Each project starts with cultural references, material research, image collection, and close observation. Themes are distilled into a focused narrative that can guide every later decision.

Research Narrative Mood Color
02 — Develop
Form & Material
Translate ideas into garments

Sketching, draping, and material experiments turn the initial concept into a coherent family of silhouettes. Proportion, movement, texture, and construction are refined through deliberate iteration.

Sketching Draping Textiles Iteration
03 — Refine
Edit & Resolve
Create a clear point of view

The strongest ideas are edited into a unified collection. Details, finishes, styling, and presentation are resolved so the final work communicates with clarity and impact.
